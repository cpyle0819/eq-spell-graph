# Zone maps: a `maps` array, not flat `map_image`/`map_legend`

Migration 061 (Oasis of Marr) and 113 (Ak'Anon) gave zone nodes flat `map_image: string` and `map_legend: {key,label}[]` fields — fine while every sourced zone was a single-level outdoor area or town. Befallen (3 dungeon levels, 2 map images since eqlwiki.com merges its Level One and Level Two into one image) and Blackburrow (3 dungeon floors, one image each) broke that: a dungeon's floors are genuinely different maps with different numbered legends, not one zone-wide key.

Fix: `zone.data.maps: { label: string | null; image: string; legend: { key: string; label: string }[] }[]`. `label` is the floor name as eqlwiki.com names it ("Levels One and Two", "Floor Three") for a multi-entry zone; `null` for the ordinary single-map zone, which still just gets a one-element array — there's no separate flat-field shape to keep in sync anymore. Migration 125 converted every zone migrated under the old flat fields (Oasis of Marr, Ak'Anon, Butcherblock Mountains, Crushbone, East Commonlands, Rivervale, Everfrost Peaks) to this shape and deleted `map_image`/`map_legend`.

UI: `zone-dossier` renders a floor-tab row above the map image whenever `maps.length > 1` (hidden entirely for the single-map case — most zones), switching both the image and that floor's own legend together on click. Tabs, image, and legend all moved to sit *above* the Bestiary/Quests row, full width, rather than sharing a row with them — a portrait dungeon map at a fixed narrow rail width (the old layout) doesn't scale to "3 tabs plus whichever floor's legend is longest."

**Reversed once the graph had enough real zones to judge by (issue #36's second-and-later batches):** the legend moved back beside the map, in a `.map-row` that pairs `<zone-map>` with `.map-legend-col` and centers the two as one unit, rather than living down with Bestiary/Quests. Every sourced map image so far tops out around 640px wide, so a legend sized to its own text (capped at `flex: 0 1 320px`, not stretched) comfortably fits next to it without approaching the cramped narrow-rail problem the original move was solving — that problem was specifically about a *fixed-width sidebar* forcing a portrait dungeon map into a slim column, not about a legend simply sitting beside a map at its natural size. Bestiary/Quests now sit in their own two-column row below, with no legend column competing for that space. Still stacks (map above, legend below, both full width) under 760px, same collapse the three-way row used before.
