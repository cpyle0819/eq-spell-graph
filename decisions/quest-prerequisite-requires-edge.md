# Quest prerequisites: a `requires` edge, and "questline" now means this

Migration 045's Cutthroat Rings (Guard Valon, West Commonlands) states its own prerequisite in prose: "Complete the Orc Picks quest (turn in 4 Orc Pawn Picks) first." At the time, Orc Picks didn't exist as a quest node in this graph, so the relationship was deliberately left as prose only — same call made again in migration 056 for Niblek's Gems' medallion-shard dependency. Once migration 057 added a real `quest:orc-picks` node, the prerequisite pointed at something real, but there was still no edge type to express it structurally.

Fix: `quest --requires--> quest` (migration 058), resolved into `QuestSummary.requires` (`src/graph.ts`) and rendered as a small caption on `quest-card.js` next to the title (e.g. Cutthroat Rings' "Requires: Orc Picks"), the same caption treatment as `questGroup`. Not a link yet — the Quests tab has no cross-card navigation/scroll-to mechanism, so it's plain text for now, same as the `questGroup` caption.

**This is the real "questline."** A linear "must finish A before B" chain between two independently-discovered quests is a fundamentally different shape from `quest_group` (Armor of Ro's seven sibling sub-quests, no order between them, all sharing one wiki page and one class/level gate). Conflating the two under one word was a live bug in this codebase: `quest_group` was originally named `quest_line`, and the same "questline" word was being used loosely for both shapes. See `decisions/quest-group-node-type.md`'s own naming-history note for the rename this prompted.

**No new node type for a `requires` chain** — unlike `quest_group`, a prerequisite pair doesn't need an umbrella overview node (no shared giver/NPC/crafting step the way Armor of Ro has Lord Searfire). It's just two ordinary `quest` nodes connected by one edge. If a chain ever grows past two links, or needs its own shared framing the way `quest_group` does, that's the point to reconsider a dedicated node type — not before.

**GitHub issue #26's own "Questlines" checklist section predates this distinction** and is actually a mix of both shapes: most of its entries (Druid Epic Quest, the various Kael/Skyshrine/Thurgadin Armor Quests, Necromancer Words) are `quest_group`-shaped — independent sibling sub-quests under one page. Plane of Sky Keys is the one entry that's genuinely `requires`-shaped — Sirran the Lunatic gates each of Plane of Sky's 7 islands behind a boss-kill-then-key-turn-in on the previous island, a real ordered chain. Worth relabeling that section (or splitting it) the next time it's touched, rather than treating "Questlines" as a reliable predictor of which modeling approach an entry needs.
