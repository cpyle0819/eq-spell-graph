# Whole-app light-source consistency pass: top-right everywhere

Every raised/inset surface across all three pages now shares one light source — top-right — instead of MacroButton/MacroSocket being a documented exception to a top-left convention used everywhere else (`.zone-card`, `.controls-panel`, `.field select`/`.tag-wrap`, `button`, `.spell-detail`, `.zone-badge`/`.hops-badge`, `.mana-bar`, plus the small circular "gem" specular highlights on `.spell-chip`, `.loading`, the range-slider thumb, and the zone-name LED dot). MacroButton/MacroSocket were left untouched — they were already correct; everything else got flipped to match them.

**The mechanical rule, derived once and applied everywhere**: for `border-color: top right bottom left`, swap the *right* and *left* values (top/bottom stay put) — a raised element's old `hi lo lo hi` (light top+left, dark bottom+right) becomes `hi hi lo lo` (light top+right, dark bottom+left); a sunken element's old `lo X X lo` becomes `lo lo X X`. For a `radial-gradient(circle at 35% 30%, ...)` sphere highlight, mirror the x-coordinate around 50% (35%→65%) so the highlight sits up-and-right of center instead of up-and-left.

**Inset `box-shadow` offsets needed an empirical check, not a guess** — CSS's inset-shadow offset-to-corner mapping isn't obvious from the property values alone, and guessing wrong would have silently shipped a bevel that contradicted its own `border-color`. Verified by rendering a plain test div with exaggerated offsets (`inset -20px -20px white, inset 20px 20px black`) and inspecting where each color actually lands: **positive `inset dx dy` paints that color on the top+left inner edge; negative paints it on bottom+right** (a diagonal pair). For the anti-diagonal pair this app actually needs (top-right/bottom-left), the offsets must have *opposite* signs: confirmed `inset -X +Y` → top+right, `inset +X -Y` → bottom+left. So a raised panel's old `inset 1px 1px white, inset -1px -1px black` (diagonal, matching its old top-left border) becomes `inset -1px 1px white, inset 1px -1px black` (anti-diagonal, matching the new top-right border) — negate the x-offset, keep the y-offset, on every directional inset shadow in the file. Shadows with a zero x-offset (e.g. `inset 0 3px 7px black`, or the mana-bar's internal seam/knob details) have no left-right asymmetry to begin with and were left alone.

Cross-checked the derived rule against `.macro-btn:active`'s existing (and, per this session's earlier fix, already-verified-correct) pressed-state shadow before trusting it on everything else — its `inset 2px -2px white, inset -2px 2px black` plugs into the same rule and correctly predicts white-bottom-left/black-top-right, matching its own code comment. Two independent confirmations (the standalone test render, and this cross-check) before touching 15+ call sites.

**One deliberate, documented exception**: `.mana-bar::after`'s per-bead specular gradient stays top-left, because it's reverse-engineered pixel-for-pixel from a zoomed capture of the real client's vitals bar (see the vitals-bar entries above) — matching the reference image takes priority over matching our own convention there, and a future pass shouldn't "fix" it back to top-right.
