The Road to Fifty

Notes for a first character, kept for anyone walking the same road since.

New to EverQuest Legends and not sure where to point your boots? This is a quick orientation — how leveling actually works, the road most people walk between one and fifty, and a few pieces of gear worth the detour. The zone picks ahead come from the EQL player community rather than eqlwiki.com's own zone tables, which are deliberately left unpublished to preserve some discovery — read them as a solid starting point, not gospel, and expect the road to shift a little as the community keeps finding its way.

Field Notes

The Trail

Lv 1–10
◆

Every class you own is leveling together while you find your footing — start anywhere near your home city. Partying up? A Magician and Cleric pair is fast and forgiving: the pet tanks, the Cleric keeps it upright.

Lv 15–25
◆

The Estate of Unrest becomes the obvious anchor — a strong experience bonus, and camps that scale right alongside you. Solusek's Eye and Mistmoore Castle carry the rest of the climb through your low twenties, and High Keep's goblin basement is worth a look too for a change of scenery.

Lv 25–40
◆

Ocean of Tears' Elite Goblin Island picks up the pace, especially with an Enchanter along to charm the reptiles. Lower Guk's Herbalist camp is worth lingering at from 40–44 — exactly the kind of single-spot camp multiclassing lets a trio hold down alone.

Lv 40–48
◆

The Hole's Docks or the Efreeti camp in Nagafen's Lair (Sol B, to anyone who's been around) close out the climb before the Planes open their doors at 46.

Off the beaten path — Najena, Permafrost, Kedge Keep, and Stonebrunt Mountains all pull solid experience too, if you'd rather skip the well-known stops. Traveling solo? The game's built for it — the easiest difficulty lets you clear content made for a group at your own pace, and a private instance is always one option away.

Notable Finds Along the Way

Soft Wicker armor — The Warrens' own gear set, leather-equivalent with a look you won't see anywhere else, weapons included.

Armor of Ro — a Paladin quest line: a Mold of Ro from the Temple of Solusek Ro and the paladins camped in Rathe Mountains, combined with a crafted Bar of Ronium at a forge.

Taskmaster's whip & earring — Crushbone's Taskmaster drops a 5/30 whip and his own brass earring.

Mold of Ro Bracer — turn two Deepwater Goblin Nets, dropped by Lake Rathetear's goblin net masters, in to David, keeper of the Bracers of Ro at the paladin camp in Rathe Mountains.

Research Aid — a repeatable turn-in to Mrysila in Northern Karana: lightstones (a willowisp drop) buy spell research tomes, greater lightstones buy the Concordance of Research.
