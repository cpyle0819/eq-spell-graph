New to EverQuest Legends and not sure where to go? This is a quick orientation — how leveling actually works, roughly where to hunt at each stage, and a few pieces of gear worth a detour along the way.

The zone-by-level picks below come from the EQL player community, not eqlwiki.com's own zone tables (which are deliberately left unpublished to preserve some discovery) — treat them as a solid starting point, not gospel, and expect them to shift as the community keeps figuring the game out.
  • All 16 classes level together in the background until level 10, when you lock in your active loadout. After that, only your lowest-level active class(es) gain further experience, so a trio naturally levels in step.
  • Auto-scribing is active through level 20, automatically granting your class's critical spells as you go.
  • Race affects leveling speed independent of faction: Dark Elf, Human, and Erudite carry no XP penalty; Ogre is -15%; Troll and Iksar are -20%.
  • Level 46 unlocks the Planes, EQL's endgame content tier.
  • 1–10: every class is leveling together, so any starting-city zone works. A Magician + Cleric duo (pet tanks, Cleric heals) is a popular fast-clear combo if you're partying.
  • 15–25: Estate of Unrest is the anchor zone — a strong XP modifier and camps that scale with you. Solusek's Eye and Castle Mistmoore carry you through the low-to-mid 20s; if those are full, High Keep's goblin basement is a solid overflow.
  • 25–40: Ocean of Tears' Elite Goblin Island (especially good with an Enchanter charming the reptiles) and Lower Guk (its Herbalist camp around 40–44) keep the pace up — the multiclass system means one trio can hold a camp that'd normally need a full group.
  • 40–48: The Hole's Docks or Sol B's Efreeti camp round out the stretch before the Planes unlock at 46.
  • Anywhere crowded: Najena, Permafrost Keep, Kedge Keep, and Stonebrunt Mountains all offer solid XP with a lot less competition for camps.
  • Playing solo? Personal instances (set to difficulty 0) let you avoid contention entirely and solo through content uncontested.
  • The Warrens has its own unique gear set — Soft Wicker armor (leather-equivalent) and matching weapons, both with a distinct look you won't see anywhere else.
  • Paladins can work toward the Armor of Ro: quest a Mold of Ro from the Temple of Solusek Ro and the paladins camped in Rathe Mountains, then combine it with a crafted Bar of Ronium at a forge.
  • Crushbone's Taskmaster drops a 5/30 whip and his own brass earring.
  • Lake Rathe's goblin net masters drop Deepwater Goblin Nets — turn two in to David for a Mold of Ro Bracer.
  • The Research Aid quest hands out a percentage of experience for every lightstone you turn in — a nice detour if you're passing through.

Sources: eqlwiki.com Zone Experience Guide, eqlwiki.com New Player Guide, eqlwiki.com Armor of Ro Quests, eqlwiki.com The Warrens, community leveling routes from the EQL player wikis.
