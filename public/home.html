Norraph

Welcome

Norraph is a companion for EverQuest Legends spellcasters, built around two things: planning spell-buying trips, and knowing what's out there to shop for in the first place. The Spell Finder and Route Finder handle the trip — tell them which classes and level range you're shopping for and where you're starting from, and they find every vendor that sells something you need, how to get there, and whether your faction standing will actually let you buy anything once you arrive.

The Class Browser is the other half: a complete reference for what every class can learn — spells, AAs, stances, invocations, and signature abilities — browsable on its own, with no shopping logistics attached. It's graph-based and faction-aware throughout: routes account for real zone connections, including boats and translocators, and results are checked against your race, class, and deity standing so a technically-closer zone that would leave you kill-on-sight never outranks a longer trip that actually works.

Pick your classes, level range, and current zone — get a ranked shopping list of nearby vendors, sorted by faction standing and travel distance.

Just need directions? Pick a starting zone and a destination and get the shortest path between them, boats and translocators included.

The complete class reference — every spell, AA, stance, invocation, and signature ability a class can learn, browsable on its own.
