Norraph is built to feel like the friend who already knows the map, not another chore to manage. Whatever brought you here, hunting down a spell, plotting the fastest safe route, or just wondering what your class is capable of down the road, there's a corner of this page for it.

Spell shopping in EverQuest Legends always comes down to the same tangle: who actually sells what you need, what zone they're hiding in, and whether they'll even deal with you once you're standing in front of them. Spells and Maps, Norraph's shopping tools, untangle it. Tell them who you're gearing up and what level range you're hunting for, and they'll surface every vendor worth the trip and the real path to reach them, boats and translocators included, then hand you the destination's own map and the lay of the land once you arrive. But a vendor's only worth the trip if they'll actually sell to you, so every result is also weighed against your race, class, and deity: a route that's two zones shorter but gets you murdered on sight never wins over the longer one that actually lets you shop.

Classes, Quests, and the Leveling Guide are the reference shelf behind all of that, no shopping trip required. Classes catalogs everything a class can learn, spells and AAs and stances and invocations and signature abilities alike; Quests tracks the quest lines actually worth running, the items they hand out, and the factions they shift; and the Leveling Guide flags the milestones worth knowing before you hit them, not after. Look them up on their own, or let them steer where Spells and Maps send you next. Either way, this is still day one.
