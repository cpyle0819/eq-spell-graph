Norraph is a companion for EverQuest Legends spellcasters. The Spell Finder and Route Finder plan out spell buying trips. Tell them which classes and level range you're shopping for and where you're starting from, and they find every vendor that sells something you need, how to get there, and whether your faction standing will actually let you buy anything once you arrive. Every route accounts for real zone connections, including boats and translocators, and is checked against your race, class, and deity standing with each vendor, so a technically closer zone that would leave you kill on sight never outranks a longer trip that actually works.

The Class Browser is a separate tool: a complete reference for what every class can learn, covering spells, AAs, stances, invocations, and signature abilities. Browse it on its own, no shopping trip required.
