Norraph is an interactive wiki for EverQuest Legends: six tools sharing one faction-aware graph, each surfacing a direct, filtered answer instead of a wiki page you'd have to read in full.

Items finds anything you need, spells included, and where to buy it, checked against your race, class, and deity so you don't get sent somewhere they won't deal with you.

Maps plots the route between two zones, boats and translocators included, and shows the destination's own map and mob levels.

Classes catalogs everything a class can learn: spells, AAs, stances, and signature abilities.

Quests tracks quest lines worth running, their rewards, and the factions they shift.

Leveling Guide flags the milestones worth knowing before you hit them.

Tradeskills lays out a trade's leveling recipes and who sells the supplies.
