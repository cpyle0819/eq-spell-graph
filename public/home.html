Norraph

Welcome

Norraph plans out spell-buying trips in EverQuest Legends. Tell it which classes and level range you're shopping for and where you're starting from, and it finds every vendor that sells something you need, how to get there, and whether your faction standing will actually let you buy anything once you arrive.

It's graph-based and faction-aware: routes account for real zone connections — including boats and translocators — and every result is checked against your race, class, and deity standing with that vendor, so a technically-closer zone that would leave you kill-on-sight never outranks a longer trip that actually works.
